About

Built by practitioners, not theorists

5et Ltd is a technology consultancy specialising in artificial intelligence. We work with enterprises navigating the transition from traditional software to AI-driven operations.

Our approach is grounded in hands-on delivery. We have designed and implemented AI solutions across industries including financial services, telecommunications, retail, and the public sector.

Founded in 2026 and registered in England & Wales, 5et operates at the intersection of enterprise architecture, machine learning, and operational excellence. We work as embedded partners with our clients — not as vendors at arm's length.

Approach

How we work

Every engagement follows a structured path from understanding to delivery.

Phase 01

Discover

We map your current state — technology landscape, data maturity, team capabilities, and business objectives. No assumptions. We start with what's true.

Phase 02

Design

Architecture decisions, technology selection, integration patterns. We produce artefacts your teams can build from — not abstract diagrams that sit in drawers.

Phase 03

Deliver

We build alongside your teams. Working software in production, with monitoring, observability, and handover documentation. The engagement ends when the system runs without us.
